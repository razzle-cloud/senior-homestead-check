Is your home getting every property tax break it qualifies for?

Each exemption has to be claimed separately with DeKalb County and with the city, and many homeowners miss one. Check your address in seconds.

City of Decatur homes only. Add a unit number for condos.

Example: part of a 2026 City of Decatur tax bill

  1. City general levy$3,962.00
  2. Homestead exemption, GH‑1−$446.00
  3. Age 65+ exemption, GH‑2not on bill
  4. You pay$3,516.00

Every exemption is its own line on the bill. When one was never applied for, it simply isn't there, and the owner pays the difference: $167 a year for GH-2 on this bill.

Work with Decatur seniors?

Approved senior centers, faith groups and neighborhood associations get a private map of which homes are missing which exemption, so they can knock on the right doors.

Bought your home in the last few years? Filing for a homestead with DeKalb at closing doesn't cover the City of Decatur. Each office needs its own application. See how to apply to both.

What the check looks at

Both offices' records

Your 2026 city tax bill lists the city exemptions you have. DeKalb County's records list the county's. The check puts them side by side.

Gaps between them

If one office has a homestead on file and the other doesn't, or DeKalb's records already show an owner's age and the city's matching exemption is missing, the check tells you which form to file.

Nothing about you

The check only uses public property records. It doesn't ask your name, age or income, and it doesn't keep the addresses people type.
