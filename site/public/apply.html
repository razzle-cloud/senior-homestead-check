How to apply

In the City of Decatur you apply to two offices. Each exemption is a separate application, and none is added automatically when you reach a new age.

City of Decatur

Covers city taxes and Decatur's school taxes (GH-1, GH-2, GH-3 and the S exemptions).

Deadline: April 1 for that year's taxes. File by March 15 to see it on the first bill.

Online: application forms on the city's homestead exemption page.

In person: Administrative Services, City Hall, 509 N. McDonough St., Decatur, 8 a.m. to 5 p.m., Monday to Friday.

Phone: 404-370-4100

DeKalb County

Covers DeKalb County taxes (the basic H1 homestead and county senior exemptions).

Deadline: DeKalb accepts applications from January 1. Filing by April 1 is safest; check the county's page for the current cut-off.

Online: the Tax Commissioner's homestead page links to online filing for basic and senior exemptions.

In person or by mail: any Tax Commissioner office, year-round.

Phone: 404-298-4000

Tips

  • Bought recently?

    Check that you filed with both offices. Filing with DeKalb at closing doesn't cover the city.

  • Turned 62, 65, 70 or 80?

    Look at the list below again. New exemptions don't start on their own.

  • Not sure?

    Call the office. Staff can tell you what's on file and which forms fit.

What to bring

  • For a basic homestead: proof you own and live in the home, such as a Georgia driver's license or ID showing the address. DeKalb also asks for vehicle registration in DeKalb.
  • For age-based exemptions: a Georgia ID or birth certificate.
  • For exemptions with an income limit: last year's income tax return, or your Social Security benefit statement (SSA-1099) if you don't file taxes.
